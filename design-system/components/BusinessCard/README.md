# BusinessCard
The card that lists one business in the directory, category pages and the home page's selected businesses.

- Logo on a white tile, 120px tall; a business without a logo file shows its initials in the serif in maroon.
- Name (`title`), one-sentence description (`small`, ink-muted), then up to three tags: sector · country · relationship.
- The whole card is one link to the business page.
